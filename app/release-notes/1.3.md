## What's new

- **Quit Completely** in the Valkey menu. While the menu-bar icon is shown, ⌘Q closes Valkey to the menu bar and keeps your servers running. Quit Completely really quits and stops your servers. With the menu-bar icon turned off, ⌘Q already quits, so the item is hidden.

## Fixes

- The menu-bar menu no longer shows ⌘Q next to **Quit Valkey.app** or ⌘, next to **Settings…**. Those shortcuts don't do what the hint suggested: ⌘Q only closes to the menu bar. ⌘, still opens Settings from the Valkey menu.
